Your Privacy Matters To Us

Aries Events Pvt. Ltd. respects the privacy of all visitors to the ROADCON EXPO website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

01

Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • City / Location

This information may be collected when you:

  • Register for the exhibition
  • Submit enquiry forms
  • Subscribe to updates
  • Contact us through the website

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect technical data such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Website usage patterns
02

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Provide event updates and information
  • Improve website functionality
  • Communicate regarding the exhibition
  • Manage registrations and enquiries

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no online platform can guarantee complete security.

05

External Links

07

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

Policy Updates
