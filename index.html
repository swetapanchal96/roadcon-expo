About ROADCON EXPO

A Dedicated Platform For Road, Highway & Infrastructure Industry

ROADCON 2026 is an extension of the successful legacy of EARTHCON EXPO, which has been a trusted platform for India's construction, earthmoving, and infrastructure industry since 2016. Over the years, EARTHCON EXPO has consistently delivered high-quality business exhibitions, connecting leading brands, contractors, engineers, developers, and key decision-makers from across the country.

With the remarkable growth and response received over the years, the platform now expands further with the introduction of ROADCON — a focused vertical dedicated exclusively to roads, highways, bridges, and smart infrastructure.

Industry Overview

India’s Road Infrastructure Is Moving Into A New Growth Era

India’s infrastructure sector is growing at a rapid pace, with significant investments in roads, highways, bridges, and smart infrastructure projects.

Government initiatives and private sector participation are accelerating the development of high-capacity road networks across the country.

With continuous focus on quality, speed, and sustainability, the industry is adopting advanced technologies and innovative construction methods.
