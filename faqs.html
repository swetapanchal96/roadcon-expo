ROADCON EXPO 2026 11-12-13 December 2026

ROADCON EXPO 2026 is a dedicated exhibition platform focused on road construction, infrastructure development, smart mobility, machinery, materials, road safety and technology solutions.

ROADCON EXPO 2026 will be held on 29-30-31 May 2026 at Eka Club, Kankaria, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Companies related to road construction equipment, machinery, asphalt, bitumen, safety products, smart infrastructure, construction materials, technology, automation and infrastructure solutions can participate as exhibitors.

You can fill out the Become an Exhibitor form on the website with your company details, contact information and stall requirement. The ROADCON team will contact you with further details.

Yes, visitors should complete online registration before attending the expo. This helps ensure smooth entry and better visitor management at the venue.

Contractors, builders, engineers, consultants, government authorities, project managers, infrastructure companies, road safety professionals, purchase teams and technology solution providers can visit ROADCON EXPO.
